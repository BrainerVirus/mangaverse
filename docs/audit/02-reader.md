# Reader and settings audit

Audited `bugfix/app-flow-audit` at `0c96b79e`, 2026-11-07. The browser probes used macOS Chromium, the existing production build, and recorded MangaDex fixtures (substituting 600×900, 1200×600 or 600×3000 PNG pages). The code in the tested build matches this commit: the intervening release commit changes no app/package source. **Windows/Electron was not exercised.** This is an audit, not a fix.

## Observed and reproduced

### 1. The settings sheet loses its background when scrolled — confirmed, high severity

Open a chapter, show controls, open Settings, scroll to the last options. At 900×700 the glass visibly stops at y=401, but Spread offset, Wide pages, Applies to and Reset continue down to y=700 on the dark scrim. Black labels become almost unreadable. At 1440×480 the whole visible lower part is uncovered. Expected: every visible row remains over the sheet surface, including when scrolling.

`packages/reader/src/components/reader-settings-sheet.tsx:210-216` applies `overflow-y-auto` to the popup. `packages/design-system/src/components/ui/sheet.tsx:30-37,85-113` makes that popup fixed, `h-full`, transparent, and puts an `h-full` glass child *inside* it. The child is one viewport high, but its content overflows; scrolling moves the child's painted background out of view with it. This is not a computed max-height: Chromium reported `max-height: none`. The fixed viewport height and background placement are the cause. `globals.css:568-573` supplies the fill and blur only on that child. No safe-area padding is applied to the sheet; the last reset button's bottom was exactly at the viewport bottom, so inset devices need a separate check.

| Viewport | Popup / glass height | Popup scroll height | Glass bottom after scroll | Last button bottom | Uncovered content |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1440×900 | 900 | 999 | 801 | 900 | 99 px |
| 900×700 | 700 | 999 | 401 | 700 | 299 px |
| 390×844 | 844 | 1087 | 601 | 844 | 243 px |
| 1440×480 | 480 | 999 | -39 | 480 | 519 px |
| 1440×1600 | 1600 | 1600 | 1600 | 999 | 0 px |

Popup: transparent, `overflow-y: auto`; glass: `rgba(255,255,255,.82)`, `blur(32px) saturate(1.8)` in the tested light/full-glass profile. Width was 384 px at 900/1440 and 293 px at 390 (`w-3/4 max-w-sm`). Screenshots of the top and scrolled sheet at all five sizes were captured during the probe; because this assignment permits only this Markdown file as an output, the temporary PNGs are not retained in the checkout. In particular, the 900×700 and 1440×480 captures showed the failure described above. Test solid/cheap glass, dark theme, scrollbar width, zoom and real safe-area insets as well.

### 2. Strip fit selection is accepted and saved but not rendered — confirmed

Set Vertical scroll, then each of Fit width, Fit height, Fit screen, Fill screen and Original size. The selected button changes, but the first three strip rows remained exactly 1008×1512 at 1440×900 for all five choices. Expected: the choice affects the strip or is clearly unavailable with an explanation. `ReaderScreen.tsx:484-490` computes fit for the paged layer, but `ReaderScrollLayer.tsx:42-72` does not pass fit to `ReaderScrollView`; the latter uses its own strip width and aspect geometry (`ReaderScrollView.tsx:213-230,639-673`). Auto correctly resolves to width in the model, but explicit alternatives are silently ignored. Strip gap is conversely dormant in paged mode. Fit width, height, screen, fill and original do have separate paged calculations (`page-fit.ts:32-69`).

### 3. Smart spread and wide-page detection lag decoded dimensions — confirmed

With initially dimensionless portrait fixtures, page through several pages, select Smart spread, and wait over four seconds: only one 504×756 page is shown. After durable reload the same page shows two 504×756 pages. A further page turn before reload still showed one. Wide 1200×600 fixtures similarly showed two paired pages before stored dimensions were reloaded; after reload, Isolate produced one 1440×720 box and Split two 720×720 half-boxes. Expected: shape-aware presentation updates as known dimensions arrive, without requiring reopen.

`spreads.ts:5-18,22-29,59-64` needs known dimensions. `use-reader-page-shape.ts:45-95` writes measurements but, after a non-strip first decision, does not merge ordinary measurements into the query/session; `use-page-dimension-writer.ts` writes them without refetching the chapter. `ReaderPagedLayer.tsx:149-151,176-181` keeps natural dimensions locally for fit but spread calculation uses chapter data. The initial split-versus-isolate screenshot was pixel-identical while shape was unknown; do not mistake that for proof that Split is always broken. After reload split markup changed; a flat image is insufficient to verify meaningful visual semantics of splitting.

### 4. Global mode appears selected while shape inference keeps strip — confirmed behavior, product/label issue

Open a dimensionless 600×3000 chapter, let Auto detect the strip, choose Applies to → All titles → Left to right. The button reads Left to right while the surface remains a strip after one second. Choose This title → Right to left and it becomes paged. The selected global value can be durable yet is not the effective mode. `resolve-reader-settings.ts:74-99` deliberately ranks shape/metadata above global (R6); `measured-page-shape.ts:93-123` retains the first-decode strip decision until a *concrete title* choice. Thus the observed behavior follows the documented priority, but the sheet does not disclose the distinction. Reconsider the wording “Changes update your defaults for every title” (`reader-settings-sheet.tsx:310-315`); show the effective mode and reason and explain title/shape precedence rather than claiming all choices are broken.

### 5. Layout changes can silently change reading direction/mode — confirmed

Set RTL, then Vertical scroll, then Double page. The panel changes Reading mode back to RTL and displays pages. `reader-settings-change.ts:18-39` intentionally adds a paged direction for *any* layout patch while strip is shown, and `ReaderScreen.tsx:436-453` remembers the last direction only if a change occurred while paged. With no session history or concrete global direction the fallback is LTR. This is intentional current behavior, but is not communicated by the Page layout control and conflicts with keeping the strip selected. The full settings page edits layout without this transformation (`settings.reader.tsx:97-109`), so the same selection has different consequences by entry point. Decide whether layout should be disabled/deferred in strip or explicitly prompt/label a transition; preserve RTL when transition is requested. Auto layout also becomes double on landscape and single on portrait, independent of the stored Auto button.

### 6. Save claim — partial reproduction, not a blanket failure

In one real browser session, choose title-scoped Single page and global Fit width; wait for durable storage, reload the reader and reopen the panel. Single page and Fit width remained selected, and the paged box remained width-fitted. The global settings page reported Fit width, while its layout remained Auto. Scope reset to This title on reopening, as designed (`reader-settings-sheet.tsx:239-255`). This verifies only those two values in this fixture, not every control, Windows, abrupt exit or concurrent writes. A separate global-versus-title-shadowing browser probe timed out and yielded no result. Title overrides can legitimately mask changed global defaults; the label does not reveal that to the user.

There are **untested save-loss hazards**, not established user reproductions: (a) a 300 ms pending edit can be lost on browser/process termination before the timer/SQLite snapshot commits; a normal client-side unmount keeps the timer (`use-coalesced-settings-write.ts:3-35`, `local-db-provider.tsx:151-157`); (b) the reader writes both layers from a whole snapshot even for a title-only edit, so a stale snapshot could overwrite a newer global write by another editor (`save-title-reader-settings.ts:27-43`); (c) the full settings page reset neither cancels the pending writer nor shares its mutation scope (`settings.reader.tsx:54-58,86-97,119-123`), allowing a delayed save to recreate the reset row; (d) reader reset cancels the pending timer but must still be tested against an in-flight save and subsequent edits (`reader.$chapterId.tsx:133-147,277-281`). Two-layer writes are sequential, not atomic: failure of the second can leave a changed global row. Schedule a controlled delayed-write test before claiming any of these happened on Windows.

## Settings matrix

All fields below are persisted in the `readerPreferences.settingsJson` global row or `title:<mangaId>` override row; the scope selector itself is session-only. **Save status:** only Single page (title) and Fit width (global) were positively checked across durable reload; no individual field was proven not to save. “Dormant” means retained for its applicable context, but the current UI does not identify that state. `ReaderSettings` schema/defaults: `packages/shared/src/reader-settings.ts:188-293,353-396`; consumers and dependencies below.

| Control / persisted field | Effect and conditions |
|---|---|
| Reading mode `readingMode`: Auto/RTL/LTR/Vertical | Auto: explicit title, majority known long page shape, long-strip tag, global, then LTR. Explicit title wins. Global can be outranked by shape; quick-panel layout selection in strip also writes a direction. |
| Page layout `pageLayout`: Auto/Single/Double/Smart spread | Paged only; Auto chooses double landscape/single portrait. Smart pairs known portrait pages, otherwise single; dimensions can lag until reopen. In quick sheet any choice in strip also changes `readingMode`. |
| Page fit `fitMode`: Auto/width/height/contain/cover/original | Paged box size/object-fit. Auto means contain paged, width strip. **All explicit choices ignored in strip**, although stored. |
| Strip/vertical gap `verticalGapPx` 0–64 | Spacing in strip; dormant paged. |
| Background `backgroundColor` | Stage color; visible around page, possibly hidden by a page filling the stage. |
| Tap zones `tapZoneLayout`: leftRight/lShaped/grid | Zone geometry. In strip left/right navigation actions map to `none`; center chrome toggle remains (`tap-zones.ts:10-25,249-268`). Examine each layout's center region before promising all are equivalent. |
| Spread offset `spreadOffset`: 0 cover alone / 1 pair first | Maps to `treatFirstPageAsCover`; only matters for paged double/smart with pairable pages. Dormant single/strip. |
| Wide pages `widePageMode`: isolate/split | Applies to known landscape pages in paged double/smart; dormant otherwise, including missing dimensions. Split renders two halves of the same page in one spread, not two sequential turns. |
| Applies to scope | This title (default on every open) or All titles. Global quick edit removes corresponding override keys for *current* title only; other titles keep overrides (`reader-settings-change.ts:48-78`). Reset title removes all overrides, not just selected scope. |
| Navigation direction `navigationDirection` | Inverts paged tap mappings; dormant for strip's left/right no-op. |
| Tap overlay `tapZoneDebugOverlay` | Visualizes regions, not navigation behavior. |
| Page transition `pageTransition` | none/fade/slide on paged transition layer; reduced motion suppresses animation; strip has no paged layer. |
| Reader chrome `chromeVisibility` | Auto / always / hidden; separate session chrome toggle. Hidden mode still needs an exit. |
| Preload ahead/behind `preloadAhead`, `preloadBehind` 0–10 | Page prefetch/decode windows; low memory caps window and data saver suppresses speculative loading. Behind is not a guarantee a strip row remains mounted. |
| Prefetch budget `prefetchBudgetBytes` | Limits new chapter-prefetch bytes (0 unlimited); dormant when no prefetch/data saver. |
| Zoom `minZoom`, `maxZoom`, `doubleTapZoom` | Gesture zoom bounds and double-tap target; schema rejects min > max. Verify strip gestures separately. |
| `pinchSensitivity`, `gestureSensitivity` | Gesture thresholds/sensitivity; require relevant touch/gesture input. |
| `imageSmoothing` | Image rendering choice; affects visible resampling, not source bytes. |
| `keepScreenOn`, `fullscreenOnOpen` | Wake lock and fullscreen request where supported/allowed; browser/platform permission can prevent effect. |
| `wheelBehavior` | none/scroll/zoom; depends on wheel input and reader mode. |
| `longStripOptimization`, `lowMemoryMode` | Bound prefetch/mount/decode resources; benefits depend on page shape, cache and workload. |
| `dataSaver` | Manual: disables prefetch and decode-ahead and narrows strip image window; saved preload settings are dormant, not erased (`to-reader-engine-settings.ts:27-31`, `use-decode-ahead.ts:32-42,59-67`). Multiple visible pages can still be needed. |
| `tilingThreshold` | Auto/4096/8192/16384: strip tall-image tiling only when threshold is crossed. |
| `displayDownscale` | Auto/off changes displayed copy, not downloaded/stored bytes; requires a sufficiently large source and supported pipeline. |
| `maxStripWidthPercent` | 40–100; 100 is responsive Auto (full phone/about 70% desktop); dormant in paged mode. |
| `showPageNumber`, `showChapterTransition` | Page count in chrome; chapter-end transition on/off. Hidden chrome also hides the count (`ReaderOverlays.tsx:83-103`). |
| `nextChapterThresholdPercent` | Scroll-past threshold in strip; dormant paged or when transition is disabled. |
| `diagnosticsEnabled` | Enables session metrics and sheet diagnostics; clear is session-only. |
| `keyboardShortcuts` | Empty means direction-aware defaults; nonempty must bind all actions uniquely; Escape/Tab reserved. |

`ReaderChrome` mode pill reports `single` for Smart spread because `resolveChromeMode` only recognizes exact `double` (`ReaderScreen.tsx:59-62`); it can thus disagree with the quick panel and actual two-page display. Rail interaction uses a 1-based UI value and converts to zero-based page (`ReaderScreen.tsx:529-550`); pointer updates are frame-coalesced and keyboard supported (`reader-progress-rail.tsx:62-107,116-160`). No rail defect was established by these probes.

## Persistence and intended state machines

**Actual route.** `fetchReaderPage` reads chapter, global row and title row (`fetch-reader-page.ts:20-47`); the repository sanitizes each field, fills absent defaults and merges overrides (`repositories/settings.ts:34-45,87-105`, `reader-settings.ts:460-497`). Quick edits merge over the query cache, optimistically paint, and coalesce for 300 ms (`reader.$chapterId.tsx:188-203`). A scoped TanStack mutation writes global if the snapshot differs and always replaces the title row; DB validation returns `AppResult`, failures are reported and queries invalidated (`reader.$chapterId.tsx:133-185`, `save-title-reader-settings.ts:6-43`). The desktop data-host exposes validated procedure calls (`packages/db/src/host/procedures.ts:473-478`, `routed.gen.ts`); web uses local persistence. On web committed snapshots ping `localStorage`, another tab syncs its handle and invalidates queries (`apps/web/app/lib/local-db.ts:456-473,500-543`, `local-db-provider.tsx:140-165`). Do not assume this proves Electron cross-window delivery: test its host notification path separately. Default query focus refetch is disabled, but the external-change subscription does its own invalidation.

**Target settings/panel machine.** `closed → loading → open(clean)` on open; surface reflects durable global+title and explicitly shows scope and effective resolution. Edit gives `open(dirty/optimistic, revision n)` and schedules a write. `save-start(n) → saving(n)`, later edits become `n+1` without being overwritten. Success advances durable revision and refetches/rebases; failure displays an actionable error, reconciles with the durable row without silently losing newer edits, and offers retry. Close must settle or explicitly preserve pending writes; reopen loads the latest durable + pending state. Reset must cancel/rebase queued edits and serialize against in-flight writes. A setting can be stored but inactive: explicitly show why. This is a proposed contract: the current UI auto-saves and has no explicit Save button or clear pending/success indication.

**Reader session.** Start with local chapter + settings + saved index, viewport and unknown/known dimensions; resolve effective mode/layout/fit, create slots and zoom. Decode may measure pages and update the shape decision without persisting an inferred mode. Navigation changes active index, visible pages and bounded prefetch/decode; progress is checkpointed separately. Settings or viewport changes rebuild the session while preserving current page (`use-reader-session.ts:30-92`); current implementation recreates zoom on rebuild, which deserves a gesture regression test. Modal state suppresses reader keyboard navigation, while chrome visibility and panel scope are independent (`ReaderScreen.tsx:327-330,411-424`). A new chapter gets a new session. A later explicit title mode must win over inferred shape, and a refetch must not clobber newer edits.

## TLA+ model proposal

Bound two titles, two windows, two values per field, two edits, one pending/in-flight write, one failure and a 0/1/2-page chapter. Variables: `globalSaved`, `titleSaved[t]`, `ui[w]`, `draftRevision[w]`, `savedRevision`, `pending[w]`, `inFlight`, `writeScope`, `queryCache[w]`, `modeSource[w]`, `effectiveMode[w]`, `shapeKnown`, `layout`, `panelOpen`, `viewportHeight`, `glassBottom`, `lastVisibleBottom`, `status[w]`, `activePage`. Actions: `Load`, `Open`, `EditTitle`, `EditGlobal`, `Queue`, `StartSave`, `SaveGlobal`, `SaveTitle`, `SaveFailure`, `CommitSnapshot`, `ExternalNotify`, `Refetch`, `Reset`, `Close`, `Reopen`, `MeasurePage`, `ResolveMode`, `Resize`, `ScrollPanel`, `Navigate`.

Check: `SavedReflectsUi` **only when quiescent and successful** (no pending/in-flight edits and refetch complete), comparing the selected layer and effective merged value, not equating a global default with an overriding title. `NoIgnoredOption`: each enabled control either affects the relevant observable engine state under a representative applicable input, or is explicitly marked inactive with a reason; saved preferences may remain dormant. `PanelCoversContent`: while open, every visible row's painted background contains its bounds after scroll and safe-area clipping. `InvalidCombinationNeverApplied`: no implicit strip/layout transition or unsupported fit without explicit disclosed policy; model any intentional dormant preference separately. Also `NewerEditNotLost`, `ResetNotResurrected`, `ExplicitTitleWins`, `NoStaleGlobalOverwrite`, `PageIndexInRange`, `FailureNotReportedSaved`. Fairness: queued writes/notifications eventually settle unless failure/termination is modeled. Explore failure between the two layer writes, reset versus queued write, two windows and shape arriving while the modal is open. This is a proposal, not a checked model.

## Ranked fixes and regression tests

1. **Panel surface/scroll ownership:** put the glass on the fixed viewport shell and scroll an inner content area, or otherwise ensure the painted background covers all scrollable content; keep close/focus, safe-area padding and blur budget. Browser test top/middle/bottom at the five sizes, assert actual painted surface covers every visible row and final padding is reachable; compare screenshots and keyboard/Escape. Old code fails at four of five sizes above.
2. **Fit/mode applicability:** implement documented strip fit semantics or disable/non-destructively defer incompatible choices with an explanation. Explicitly disclose that choosing layout leaves strip if that policy remains; show effective Auto mode/source and title/global precedence. Tests assert geometry changes or disabled/reason for every fit × mode and that the user's RTL is preserved. Old strip fit test fails.
3. **Shape propagation:** merge valid decoded dimensions into the session immediately (including neighbors) while keeping the shape vote stable and persistence asynchronous; avoid a refetch that discards optimistic settings or page position. Test dimensionless portraits/wide pages, decode then Smart/Isolate/Split in the *same* open session, and compare after reopen; test one tall credits page and user override. Old code needs reopen.
4. **Durability and concurrency:** persist scoped patches/transactions rather than blindly rewriting both stale layers, coordinate reset and in-flight writes, expose pending/error/retry and define close/quit durability. Test controlled delayed title/global writers, two titles/windows, second-layer failure, edit→reset inside 300 ms, write failure/refetch with a newer edit, abrupt close and restart. These are hypothesis-driven tests, not claimed reproduced defects.
5. **Control clarity:** represent Smart spread in the chrome, label dormant spread/gap/tap/preload options, and distinguish saved preference from inferred effective mode. Test Auto landscape/portrait, single/double, cover/offset, wide/unknown shape, RTL/invert, data saver and reduced motion with behavior assertions rather than only pressed buttons.

## Checks, confidence and gaps

- Temporary Playwright probes on the existing production server build: panel geometry **5/5 passed**, settings probes **5/6 passed** (the shadowing probe timed out), additional shape/slider probes **5/5 passed**. These exploratory scripts logged observations rather than asserting all desired product behavior. Servers launched by those probes exited. The test PNGs and scripts were temporary and are not retained under the single-file output constraint.
- `pnpm --filter @app/reader exec vitest run src/resolve-reader-settings.test.ts src/reader-settings-change.test.ts src/measured-page-shape.test.ts src/spreads.test.ts src/page-fit.test.ts`: **5 files, 103 tests passed**. This does not cover the browser discrepancies above.
- High confidence: sheet geometry, strip-fit no-op, shape-lag and global-mode/shape mismatch in the tested Chromium fixture. Medium: concurrency hazards inferred from source. Low/unverified: which hazard explains the Windows report; real Electron IPC and SQLite durability, GPU glass fallback, scaling/DPI, scrollbar, native color picker, abrupt quit, cross-window/session and all combinations must be reproduced on Windows. No blanket “settings do not save” conclusion is supported.
